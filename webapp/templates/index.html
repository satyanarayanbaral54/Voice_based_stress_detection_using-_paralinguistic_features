Paralinguistic Acoustic Feature Pipeline

Unlike transcription-based systems, our models adhere strictly to paralinguistic acoustic properties. The semantic spoken words are completely discarded to prevent linguistic or lexical bias:

MFCCs (1–13): Vocal tract spectral envelope & timbre Pitch F0 (YIN): Vocal fold vibration frequency & intonation tension RMS Energy: Vocal loudness dynamics and intensity Jitter (local): Cycle-to-cycle pitch frequency perturbation Shimmer (local): Cycle-to-cycle vocal amplitude perturbation
